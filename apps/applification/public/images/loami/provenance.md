# Loami product artwork

Copied from the maintained assets in the Loami repository, also served by its Storybook, on 30 September 2026.

- `loami-mark.svg` is the owner-approved chat-bubble mark with ivory eyes and a green sprout.
- `loami-wordmark-light.svg` uses Kalam Bold outlines with a custom sprout. The font's SIL Open Font License accompanies this file.
- `loami-3d-hello.webp` is a 448px-wide WebP derivative of the owner-approved ivory companion, generated with ImageGen from the owner's character reference. The original PNG remains in the Loami repository.
- `loami-presence.riv` is Loami's maintained animated presence, copied from `apps/web/public/assets/loami/loami-presence.riv`. Its Presence view model provides idle, listening, thinking and speaking states.
- `felucian-garden-spread.webp` is the household-uploaded image retained by Loami for the Felucian Garden Spread recipe. The owner selected this recipe and image for the product page on 30 September 2026.

Canonical provenance is recorded in the Loami repository at `apps/web/public/assets/provenance.md`.
